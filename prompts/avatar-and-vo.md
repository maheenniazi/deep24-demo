# Avatar + Voiceover pack

## 1. Avatar character (keep it CONSISTENT)
Generate ONE character still first, lock it, and reuse it (same seed / "character
reference" / image-to-image) for every clip so the face doesn't drift.

**Image-gen prompt (character still):**
> Vertical 9:16 portrait. A young woman with long dark hair, sitting in a cozy warm-lit room,
> natural woven rattan / bamboo screen background, a dried pampas/flower arrangement to one side,
> small amber glass bottle and an open book on the desk in front of her. She wears a black
> off-shoulder top. She is holding a small plush bunny toy up near her mouth like a microphone
> (a fuzzy windscreen on top of it). Soft natural daylight, shallow depth of field, phone-camera
> selfie look, relaxed friendly expression, looking at camera. Realistic, photographic.

Generate 3–4 variations of the same character for the intro beats (mouth open/talking, slight
head tilt, holding the bunny-mic). Keep wardrobe + background identical.

**Then animate + lip-sync** each still to the matching VO line using an avatar/video model
(or test CapCut's built-in AI avatar / digital-human first — if quality's fine, it's one tool).

---

## 2. Full voiceover script (for TTS or your own voice)
Generate this as ONE consistent voice. Use the **intro lines** for the avatar's lip-sync, and
the rest as VO played over your screen recordings. Auto-caption the final audio in CapCut.

**Intro (avatar ON camera):**
1. "here's how I created this"
2. "VIRAL vlog intro using the train door edit"
3. "flash overlay effect — and the…"
4. "let's go!"

**Tutorial (VO over screen recordings):**
5. "start by filming a train door as it opens"
6. "you can also use a regular door, a window, or anything else that reveals the scene"
7. "import that footage and your main video into CapCut, or any editing software"
8. "place the train clip on top as an overlay"
9. "select remove background, choose custom removal, and remove everything visible through the window"
10. "and to take it a step further, add some text"
11. "then open up the layer controls and move the text behind the train door clip"
12. "now let's create the flash transition"
13. "choose any clip you're in, turn the duplicate into an overlay"
14. "then extend it slightly so it begins before the main clip"
15. "next select remove background, and remove the subject from the rest of the shot"
16. "move that layer behind the overlay, then split the overlay"
17. "finally apply the white flash video effect"
18. "and add the glitch and camera flash sounds to elevate it even more"
19. "put it all together and you'll have something like this"

**TTS voice direction:** young, warm, casual, upbeat — "soft-girl vlogger" tone, slightly
breathy, conversational pace. (ElevenLabs or similar.)

---

## 3. Which beats are which
- **Avatar on camera:** lines 1–4 (~7 seconds).
- **Everything else:** VO over your CapCut screen recordings + the door/reveal footage.
