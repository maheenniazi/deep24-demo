# Build-A clip prompts (door · reveal · hero)

You need 3 short clips for the train-door effect. **Easiest = film 2 of them yourself**
(the reference literally says "film a train door"). AI-generate any you can't film.
Each clip only needs to be ~2–4 seconds. Export/keep 9:16 vertical.

---

## Clip 1 — THE DOOR (the overlay)
This becomes the top layer; you'll erase the window glass so the scene shows through.
You need clean door shapes with clear windows.

**Easiest:** film a real door/elevator/train/bus door **opening**, shot straight-on from inside.
A window or sliding glass door works too.

**AI prompt (text-to-video, 9:16):**
> POV from inside a subway train, facing a pair of closed train doors with two large rounded
> windows. Daylight outside. The doors slowly slide apart to reveal the platform. Static
> handheld phone shot, realistic, natural light, vertical 9:16.

> Tip: it doesn't matter what's *outside* the windows — you'll replace it. You just need crisp
> door frames + windows and a clean "open" motion.

---

## Clip 2 — THE REVEAL SCENE (what shows through the windows)
This sits *behind* the door layer.

**Easiest:** use your own concert/event/city footage, or free stock (Pexels/Pixabay).

**AI prompt (text-to-video, 9:16):**
> A vibrant nighttime concert from the crowd: huge stage washed in pink and purple light,
> silhouetted crowd with phones raised, fireworks bursting above the stage, energetic
> atmosphere. Cinematic, realistic, vertical 9:16.

> Swap the subject freely — beach, city skyline, fireworks, anything eye-catching. The brief
> allows any topic.

---

## Clip 3 — THE HERO SHOT (payoff after the flash)
The main clip the whole edit lands on.

**Easiest:** film yourself (or anyone) from behind on a platform as a train/bus passes.

**AI prompt (text-to-video, 9:16):**
> A young woman with long dark hair in a black outfit, seen from behind, standing on a subway
> platform, one arm raised adjusting her hair, as a train rushes past in motion blur behind her.
> Cinematic, moody, shallow depth of field, realistic, vertical 9:16.

**Optional (match your avatar):** use image-to-video with a full-body still of your Gemini
character so the hero is the same person. Harder to keep consistent — only if you have time.

---

## After generating
Drop all three in `assets/` with clear names and push:
- `assets/clip-door.mp4`
- `assets/clip-reveal.mp4`
- `assets/clip-hero.mp4`

Then we build the effect in CapCut (and you screen-record it for the tutorial b-roll).
